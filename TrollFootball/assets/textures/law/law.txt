GAME MODES
----------------------------------------
1. PLAY VS AI
- Player 1 plays against the computer-controlled opponent.

2. PLAY VS PLAYER
- Two human players play against each other.

----------------------------------------
CHARACTER SELECTION
----------------------------------------
- Before the match starts, players can select their characters.

----------------------------------------
CONTROLS
----------------------------------------
PLAYER 1:
- Move Left: A
- Move Right: D
- Jump: W
- Kick: J
- Dash: C

PLAYER 2:
- Move Left: Left Arrow
- Move Right: Right Arrow
- Jump: Up Arrow
- Kick: Numpad 0
- Dash: Numpad 1

----------------------------------------
MATCH RULES
----------------------------------------
- Each match lasts 1 minute 30 seconds (90 seconds).
- The player who scores the most goals when the time runs out wins.
- If both players have the same number of goals, the match ends in a draw.
- Random meteors can fall during the match.
- Meteors can hit players and temporarily stun them.
- Players must avoid the falling meteors while trying to score goals.

----------------------------------------
PAUSE
----------------------------------------
- Press ESC during the match to open the Pause Menu.
- The Pause Menu allows the player to resume the match or leave the match.
